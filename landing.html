How it works

Three scans to lower prices

Go to your Subscribe & Save subscriptions page, scan each side, and let the extension find your savings.

1

Scan personal subscriptions

Go to your Subscribe & Save page on your personal account and click Scan Prices. The extension fetches the S&S price for every subscription.

2

Switch and scan business

Switch accounts to your business account's Subscribe & Save page and scan again. The extension automatically merges your personal products in, so it gets business prices for everything.

3

Switch back and re-scan personal

Return to your personal account and scan one more time. This picks up prices for any business-only subscriptions, giving you complete coverage for both sides.

4

Compare and migrate

Click Compare to see every product side by side with annual savings. Use Bulk Subscribe to move your subscriptions to whichever account has the better price.

Only have subscriptions on one account? You only need two scans — scan your subscriptions, switch accounts, scan again, and you're ready to compare.

Scans take roughly 3 seconds per subscription to avoid Amazon rate limits. A typical account with 40 items takes about 2 minutes; larger accounts with hundreds of items may take longer.
